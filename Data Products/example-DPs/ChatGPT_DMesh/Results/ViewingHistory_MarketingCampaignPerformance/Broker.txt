**Compatibility Report**

**Mismatches**

The following mismatches have been identified by both data product owners:

* Data Formats: Incompatible formats (Kafka-based distribution vs. SQL table)
* Schemas: Different schema structures (logical schemas identical but physical schemas differ)
* Transformation Logic: Different transformation logic (ad log extracts, Google Ads and Meta API ingest vs. Flink-based stream processing)
* Control Port Policies: Different control port policies (campaign ID validity and consistency vs. managed policy)
* Syntax: Potential issues with adapting Kafka-based distribution to SQL-based distribution
* Semantics: Different focus (customer viewing behavior vs. marketing campaign performance)
* Cataloging: Different metadata and schema (catalog records and data assets vs. catalog records and marketing domain glossary)
* Data sources: Different data sources (logs from internal content player systems vs. clickstream and attribution data from ad platforms)
* Institutional knowledge and governance policies: Different institutional knowledge and governance policies
* Metadata management: Different metadata management requirements
* Estimated value and use cases: Different estimated values and use cases

**Compatibility Analysis**

The mismatches identified are a mix of negotiable and non-negotiable issues. The differences in data formats, schemas, and transformation logic are significant and require careful consideration. However, the differences in control port policies, syntax, and semantics can be addressed through additional processing, transformation logic, and policy checks.

**Recommendations**

To resolve the mismatches, the following recommendations are made:

* Develop a data transformation layer to convert Data Product A's Kafka-based distribution to a format compatible with Data Product B's SQL table
* Adapt the schema structures of the two data products to work together, ensuring compatibility and consistency
* Align the transformation logic of Data Product A and Data Product B to ensure seamless integration
* Align or adapt the control port policies of the two data products
* Develop a data integration layer to combine insights from both data products
* Conduct a thorough analysis to understand how customer viewing behavior relates to marketing campaign performance
* Update the cataloging system to accommodate the different metadata and schema of the two data products
* Apply institutional knowledge and governance policies of Data Product B to Data Product A
* Implement metadata management to ensure consistency across the data mesh
* Re-evaluate estimated value and use cases of the combined data product

**Conclusion**

Based on the analysis, the two data products are not entirely compatible due to the non-negotiable mismatches. However, with careful consideration and implementation of the recommended solutions, the compatibility between Data Product A and Data Product B can be improved, enabling a more seamless integration and maintaining the integrity of the data mesh.
