**Mismatches**

A summary of the mismatches identified by both data product owners:

* Incompatible data formats (snapshot table vs. Python dictionary)
* Incompatible schemas (different schema definitions)
* Different domains and ownership (Sales_Domain vs. Marketing_Domain)
* Different transformation logic (Spark ETL pipelines vs. ad log extracts, Google Ads and Meta API ingest)
* Different input/output ports and data contracts
* Different governance and policies across domains

**Compatibility Analysis**

An analysis of the compatibility of the two data products based on the mismatches:

The non-negotiable mismatches (data formats, schemas, and domains) pose significant challenges to integrating the data products directly. However, the negotiable mismatches (input/output ports, transformation logic, and ports and governance) suggest opportunities for data exchange or integration at a higher level.

**Recommendations**

Recommendations for resolving the mismatches:

1. **Data Format Standardization:** Establish a common data format standard across the organization to facilitate integration.
2. **Schema Harmonization:** Develop a unified schema that can accommodate both data products, ensuring data consistency and integrity.
3. **Domain Alignment:** Identify business requirements that justify integrating data products from different domains, ensuring a clear understanding of the benefits and challenges involved.
4. **Port Adaptation:** Adapt the input/output ports, transformation logic, and control ports to work together seamlessly.
5. **Data Format Conversion:** Convert DP4's Python dictionary to a structured data format compatible with DP3.
6. **Governance and Policy Alignment:** Establish common governance and policies across domains to facilitate integration.

**Conclusion**

The overall compatibility of the two data products is conditional upon resolving the non-negotiable mismatches. If the non-negotiable mismatches are not resolved, the data products are not compatible. However, if the negotiable mismatches are adapted to work together seamlessly, the data products are compatible.
