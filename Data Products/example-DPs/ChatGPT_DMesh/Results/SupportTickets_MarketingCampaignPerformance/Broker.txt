**Compatibility Report**

**Mismatches**
The following mismatches have been identified by both data product owners:

* Mismatch 1: The refresh rates and distribution mechanisms differ between the two data products.
* Mismatch 2: The schemas of the two data products are distinct, with different structures and content.
* Mismatch 3: The control ports and enforcement logic in both data products serve different purposes.
* Mismatch 4: The transformation logic in both data products is distinct.
* Mismatch 5: The language of the marketing campaign performance evaluation tool can be adapted to English, if necessary.
* Mismatch 6: The data security and integrity of the Support Tickets data product must be ensured, and the marketing campaign performance tool must adhere to these security and integrity standards.

**Compatibility Analysis**
Based on the provided mismatches, an analysis of the compatibility of the two data products has been conducted. The mismatches are negotiable, meaning that the data products can be made compatible with some adjustments.

**Recommendations**
Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are provided to resolve the mismatches:

1. Develop a common data model that accommodates both campaign performance metrics and support ticket data.
2. Create a new data asset that combines the strengths of both data products.
3. Establish a new control port that enforces policies for both campaign ID validity and sensitive language redaction.
4. Adapt the language of the marketing campaign performance evaluation tool to English, if necessary, and extend or adapt the institutional knowledge base of the Support Tickets data product.
5. Ensure that the marketing campaign performance evaluation tool adheres to the data security and integrity standards of the Support Tickets data product.
6. Modify the transformation logic to accommodate the integration of both data products.
7. Harmonize the security measures and governance policies of the two products for integration.
8. Adapt or transform the schemas of both data products for compatibility.
9. Develop a data integration plan that addresses the differences in data formats, schemas, and control ports.
10. Establish a common institutional knowledge base that accommodates both data products.
11. Develop a data quality plan to ensure data integrity and security throughout the integration process.

**Conclusion**
Based on the analysis and recommendations, the conclusion is that the two data products are compatible. The mismatches identified are negotiable, and with the suggested solutions, the data products can be integrated to provide a comprehensive view of customer behavior and enable sentiment analysis and service improvement.
